# French avocat advertising rules — what Google Ads copy must respect

French lawyers may advertise (publicité is allowed since the 2014 reform of the RIN — Règlement Intérieur National du Conseil National des Barreaux), but under real constraints. This isn't a suggestion — a client who is an avocate can face disciplinary proceedings from her Barreau (here, Barreau de Paris) over misleading or non-compliant ad copy. Check every draft headline/description against this list before finalizing.

## Hard rules

- **Honest and not misleading (loyale et non trompeuse).** No exaggerated, unverifiable, or vague superlative claims: avoid "meilleur avocat", "n°1", "expert incontesté". Prefer verifiable facts already on the site (price, duration, scope of the offer).
- **No promise of result.** Never write copy implying a guaranteed outcome: no "garantie de gain de cause", "vous gagnerez", "résultat garanti" — especially risky for the `contentieux-btp.html` litigation offer, where outcomes are inherently uncertain. Frame around process and expertise ("défense de vos intérêts en référé expertise"), not outcome.
- **No comparison or disparagement of other lawyers.** No "meilleur que les autres avocats", no implicit comparison ("contrairement à d'autres cabinets...").
- **"Spécialiste" is a protected/regulated term.** Only use "avocat spécialiste en [domaine]" if the lawyer holds the actual *certificat de spécialisation* delivered by the CNB for that domain. Otherwise use accurate non-protected phrasing: "avocate spécialisée en droit des marques" is commonly used in practice as a plain-language description of focus (not the protected certification), but to stay safest prefer wording like "avocate intervenant en...", "avocate dédiée à...", or reference concrete experience/track record instead of claiming the formal title.
- **Price transparency, if a price is mentioned.** If ad copy states a price (as several of this site's offers do: 200€, 900€ HT, 4 500€ HT, 7 000€ HT), it must match what's actually charged and displayed on the landing page, including whether it's HT or TTC — don't let an ad round or simplify a price in a way that creates a mismatch with the landing page.
- **Respect for professional secrecy and dignity.** No client testimonials naming or clearly identifying a real case/client without consent, no sensationalizing litigation ("scandale", "combat", "guerre judiciaire") in a way that undermines the dignity of the profession.
- **Clear identification as a lawyer, not a generic service.** Ads should make clear this is legal counsel from an avocate au Barreau de Paris, not a generic consulting or template service — this also happens to raise trust and Quality Score.

## Practical implications for ad copy in this account

- Favor facts already validated by the client's own site copy (durations, prices, scope, "Barreau de Paris") over generic persuasive lawyer-marketing language — it's both safer and more differentiated.
- For the BTP litigation campaign specifically, keep language procedural and factual ("Défense des artisans et sous-traitants en référé expertise judiciaire") rather than emotionally charged or outcome-promising.
- When in doubt about a specific headline, flag it to the user rather than guessing — this reference covers the general rules, not every edge case, and the client (as the avocate) is the one accountable to her Ordre des avocats.
